About this system

A multi-output deep neural network (TensorFlow/Keras) trained on rainfall, temperature, humidity, wind speed, river level, soil moisture, seismic magnitude, and pressure readings. It predicts both a risk level (Low/Medium/High/Critical) and a likely disaster type (flood, cyclone, earthquake, drought, or none), then feeds high-risk predictions into the alert pipeline for automated notification.
